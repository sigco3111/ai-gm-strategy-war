# Regions — Manchuria

## [CHUNK: location -- Manchuria North (Yalu Headwaters)]
# triggers: manchuria, yalu headwaters, 동부여, sushen, northeast
# priority: 6
**The Manchurian North** — the upper Yalu River basin and the eastern Manchurian forests, homeland of the **Sushen (수신)** tribes and the declining **Eastern Buyeo (동부여)**. This is a frontier of dense forest, fast rivers, and small tribal polities — a poor match for cavalry warfare, an excellent match for the small-unit Goguryeo infantry tradition.

**Geography**: boreal forest, short summers, long winters. Agriculture is marginal; the population lives by hunting, fishing, and small-scale farming.

**Strategic value**: A Goguryeo **campaign into the Sushen forests** would extend Goguryeo's reach to the Russian Pacific coast. The tribes here are **tributary**, not subjects — they acknowledge Goguryeo's overlordship in exchange for trade goods and peace. A foolish invasion would turn them into enemies and provoke the Eastern Buyeo.

**Current status (391)**: Tributary status. Tribute flows north; trade goods flow south. The Sushen are not a threat, but they are not allies either.

## [CHUNK: location -- Eastern Buyeo Marches]
# triggers: eastern buyeo, 동부여, dongbuyeo, 동변
# priority: 6
**The Eastern Buyeo Marches** — the eastern frontier of Goguryeo, bordering Eastern Buyeo. The **동변 (Dongbyeol, "Eastern Frontier")** fortress system mirrors the Western Frontier and runs from the Yalu headwaters southeast toward the coast.

**Geography**: forested hills, river valleys. Eastern Buyeo's capital was in this region; the Buyeo people have lived here for centuries.

**Strategic value**: Eastern Buyeo is **weak and tempting**. A Goguryeo campaign could absorb Eastern Buyeo entirely — but it would also extend Goguryeo's supply lines deep into unfamiliar territory. The player must weigh expansion against consolidation.

**Current status (391)**: Eastern Buyeo is a tributary neighbor. Tension is low but not absent. Gwanggaeto's historical record shows an Eastern Buyeo campaign in 410; the seed carries the precedent but does not require it.
