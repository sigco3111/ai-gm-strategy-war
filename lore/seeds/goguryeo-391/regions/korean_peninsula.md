# Regions — Korean Peninsula

## [CHUNK: location -- Han River Basin]
# triggers: han river, 한강, wiryeseong, 서울, baekje frontier, south
# priority: 8
**The Han River Basin (한강유역)** — the fertile central peninsula, currently held by **Baekje** (with Wiryeseong, modern Seoul, as its historic capital). This is the most agriculturally productive region on the peninsula and the primary objective of any Goguryeo southern campaign.

**Geography**: river lowlands, surrounded by mountains. Excellent rice and millet agriculture. Population centers include Wiryeseong (Baekje's former capital until 396), Pungmal (풍말, modern Seoul area), and several fortress towns.

**Strategic value**: Whoever holds the Han River basin holds the **food economy of the peninsula**. A Goguryeo conquest of this region would shift the strategic balance decisively in Goguryeo's favor. Conversely, Baekje's hold on this region is the foundation of its current dominance.

**Current status (391)**: Held by Baekje. Fortified. The historical record shows Gwanggaeto will eventually take it (396), but the seed does not guarantee this outcome.

## [CHUNK: location -- Liao Frontier]
# triggers: liao river, 요하, western frontier, manchuria, yalu
# priority: 7
**The Liao Frontier (요하 변경)** — the western marches of Goguryeo, along the Liao River basin. This is the **contested zone** with Later Yan. The Yalu and Liao rivers roughly demarcate the border between Goguryeo's sphere and the Xianbei lands; the actual frontier shifts with each war.

**Geography**: rolling hills and river valleys, suitable for cavalry warfare. The Xianbei favor this terrain. Korean fortresses here must be heavily garrisoned.

**Strategic value**: A Goguryeo **stronghold network** along the Liao is the only defense against a Xianbei invasion. The famous **서변 (Seobyeol, "Western Frontier")** system — a chain of fortresses from the Yalu north to the Sungari — was built up over generations specifically to hold this line.

**Current status (391)**: Contested. Gogugyang's 385 invasion of Later Yan suggests Goguryeo has tried to project power here. The line is fluid.
