# Regions — Gungnae (Capital)

## [CHUNK: location -- Gungnae Seong]

# priority: 9
**Gungnae Seong (國內城)** — the capital of Goguryeo, a fortified mountain city on the middle Yalu River (modern Ji'an, Jilin). The capital moved here from Jolbon/Hwando in the 3rd century; in 391 it is the political and military heart of the kingdom.

**Geography**: Gungnae sits in a bend of the Yalu, surrounded by mountains. The fortress walls are substantial but the surrounding terrain limits agriculture. The capital's food comes largely from tribute and trade with the southern provinces.

**Strategic value**: As the seat of the king, Gungnae is the **objective of any invasion**. The city's fortress and the surrounding terrain make it defensible, but a determined siege could starve it. The player must always consider Gungnae's defense in any war plan.

**Current status**: Stable. The court is here, the treasury is here, the crown prince (Georyeon) was likely born here.
