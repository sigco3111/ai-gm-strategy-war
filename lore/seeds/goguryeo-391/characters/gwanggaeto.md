# Characters — Gwanggaeto

## [CHUNK: character -- Gwanggaeto the Great]
# triggers: gwanggaeto, 광개토대왕, taewang, yeongnak, goguryeo king, 고담덕
# priority: 9
**Gwanggaeto the Great (광개토대왕, 廣開土大王, "Great King Who Expands the Territory")** — born **고담덕 (高談德, Go Damdeok)** in 374 CE, ascended the throne of Goguryeo in **May 391 at age 17**. Era name: **영락 (永樂, "Eternal Rejoicing")**. Title: **태왕 (太王, "Supreme King")** — an imperial designation claiming parity with the Chinese emperors.

Gwanggaeto is the **player character** in the goguryeo-391 seed. He has been **crown prince since January 386** (age 12), giving him five years of preparation. He has read the histories, studied the generals, listened to the surviving veterans of the 371 defeat, and watched his father wage war fruitlessly against Later Yan and Baekje.

His known traits (per historical record, as the seed's starting point):
- **Ambitious** — he will not be content with his grandfather's defeat.
- **Prudent** — he does not rush; he waits for the right moment.
- **Tactically brilliant** — historical record credits him with consistently outmaneuvering larger forces.
- **Culturally confident** — his adoption of the 태왕 title is a deliberate political statement of Goguryeo's independence from China.

The seed does not protect him from history. He may die young, as he historically did (age 38), or live long. His era name 영락 ("Eternal Rejoicing") may be ironic, literal, or both.

## [CHUNK: character -- Gwanggaeto Education]
# triggers: goguryeo education, 태학, crown prince, military training, 거련
# priority: 6
Gwanggaeto's education, as a prince and crown prince (386–391), included:

- **Taehak (태학)** — the national school established by Sosurim, where the royal children studied with the sons of the great nobles. This was both education and political networking.
- **Military apprenticeship** — he accompanied his father Gogugyang on the 385 and 386 campaigns against Later Yan and Baekje, learning the art of war in the field.
- **Buddhist tutelage** — the monks of the newly-established temples served as teachers of literacy, history, and diplomacy. Gwanggaeto's Confucian-Buddhist education shaped his worldview.
- **Crown Prince Georyeon (거련, 巨連)** — Gwanggaeto's son and heir, born c. 391. A child at game start. His upbringing is a long-term investment.

The GM may add, subtract, or rename any of these figures in play. The seed is a starting point, not a locked historical script.
