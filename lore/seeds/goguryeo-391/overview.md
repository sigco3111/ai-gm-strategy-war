# Goguryeo 391 — Overview

> The year is **391 CE. Spring.**
>
> **You are 고담덕 (高談德), known to history as 광개토대왕 (廣開土大王, "Gwanggaeto the Great").** Seventeen years old. Crown prince since 386, now king by your father's death. Your era begins: **영락 (永樂, "Eternal Rejoicing")**. You have claimed the title **태왕 (太王, "Supreme King")** — an imperial title that places you as an equal to the Chinese emperors, not their vassal.
>
> You rule from **국내성 (國內城, Gungnae Seong)** — the fortified mountain capital on the Yalu River, modern Ji'an, Jilin. Your grandfather 고국원왕 was killed by Baekje in 371; your father 고국양왕 spent his reign at war. You inherit a kingdom recovering from defeat, with a strong military but bruised legitimacy.

## The World in 391

The Korean peninsula is divided among **three kingdoms** and a constellation of smaller polities:

- **고구려 (Goguryeo)** — the kingdom you rule. Manchuria + northern peninsula. Recovering.
- **백제 (Baekje)** — under king 아신 (Asin). Currently the dominant power. The threat.
- **신라 (Silla)** — southeastern peninsula. Smaller, but rich and stable. A natural ally.
- **가야 (Gaya)** — a confederacy of small maritime states in the south. Useful as a port.
- **동부여 (Eastern Buyeo)** — northeast, formerly a rival of Goguryeo. Weakened; possible target.

To the west, beyond the Yalu, sits the **Xianbei** state of **후연 (Later Yan)** under the Murong clan — currently the strongest single power in the region. To their south, the **수신 (Sushen)** tribes hold the forests. The **Wa** (early Japanese archipelago) occasionally sends raiders across the sea.
