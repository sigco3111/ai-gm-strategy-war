# Factions — Baekje

## [CHUNK: faction -- Baekje]
# triggers: baekje, 백제, paekche, kudara
# priority: 9
**Baekje (백제)** — the principal southern rival. Under king **Asin (아신왕, 392–405)**, Baekje had reached its zenith by 391. King Geunchogo (근초고왕, r. 346–375) had decisively defeated Goguryeo in 371, slaying King Gogukwon and sacking the old capital of Pyongyang. Asin inherited this momentum — a strong military, a wealthy southern coast, and an aggressive diplomatic posture.

Baekje's **strength** is its position: the fertile Han River basin and the southwestern coast, with maritime trade routes extending to China and the Wa archipelago. Its **weakness** is overextension — Asin must defend against Goguryeo in the north, Silla in the east, and maintain trade dominance over the southern sea lanes. A two-front war is Baekje's nightmare.

In 391, Baekje controls the Han River valley and the old Wiryeseong (modern Seoul area) region. It has not yet pushed north into the Yalu corridor. The player (as Gwanggaeto) is positioned to threaten its northern frontier.

**Ruler**: Asin (아신). Asin's reign will see both victories and a final catastrophic defeat at the hands of Gwanggaeto in 396 — but the seed does not protect him from that fate. The dice fall where they fall.

## [CHUNK: faction -- Baekje Aftermath of 371]
# triggers: geunchogo, 371, gogukwon, 평양, sack, legacy
# priority: 7
The **371 defeat of Goguryeo** is the founding trauma of Goguryeo's recent memory. King Geunchogo of Baekje led an army north, defeated Goguryeo in battle, killed King Gogukwon in combat, and sacked the capital at Pyongyang. The royal family fled north; the king of the time (Sosurim) was a regent who rebuilt from Gungnae. The lesson was hard: Baekje, not Goguryeo, was the dominant power in the peninsula.

The 371 trauma shaped a generation of Goguryeo policy: appeasement under Sosurim, military reform under Sosurim, and Gogugyang's failed attempt to avenge the defeat. By 391, the wound is twenty years old but unhealed. The player inherits both the memory and the unfinished vendetta.
